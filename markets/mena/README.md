# Bozor paketi: Yaqin Sharq va Shimoliy Afrika (BAA, KSA, GCC) (`mena`)

- **Standart valyuta:** `AED` (qo'llab-quvvatlanadi: `AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, EGP, USD`)
- **To'lov usullari:** Mada (KSA), Apple Pay, Visa/Mastercard, Cash on Delivery (COD)
- **BNPL provayderlar:** Tabby, Tamara, Postpay
- **Narx anker shabloni:** `Split into 4 interest-free payments of {installment} with {bnpl} (Sharia-compliant)`
- **Asosiy voronkalar:**
  - Reklama -> Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) -> Arabic/English Concierge Sales -> Payment Link / Tabby
  - Reklama -> Bilingual (AR/EN) E-Commerce Store -> Tabby/Tamara/Apple Pay Checkout
- **Xabar kanallari:** WhatsApp Business (Primary), Instagram DM, Snapchat, TikTok
- **Maxfiylik va reklama qonunchiligi:**
  - Saudiya PDPL (Shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni himoya qilish qonuni)
  - BAA 2021-yil 45-sonli Federal Farmon-Qonun (PDPL)
  - UAE Media Council / KSA GCAM reklama litsenziyalash talablari
- **Tillar (BCP 47):** `ar, en` (RTL: `ar`)
- **Tekshirilgan sana:** `2026-10-21`
