# Market Pack: Middle East & North Africa (UAE, KSA, GCC) (`mena`)

- **Default Currency:** `AED` (Supported: `AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, EGP, USD`)
- **Payment Methods:** Mada (KSA), Apple Pay, Visa/Mastercard, Cash on Delivery (COD)
- **BNPL Providers:** Tabby, Tamara, Postpay
- **Price Anchor Template:** `Split into 4 interest-free payments of {installment} with {bnpl} (Sharia-compliant)`
- **Primary Funnels:**
  - Ad -> Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) -> Arabic/English Concierge Sales -> Payment Link / Tabby
  - Ad -> Bilingual (AR/EN) E-Commerce Store -> Tabby/Tamara/Apple Pay Checkout
- **Messaging Channels:** WhatsApp Business (Primary), Instagram DM, Snapchat, TikTok
- **Privacy & Ad Regulations:**
  - Saudi PDPL (Personal Data Protection Law)
  - UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL)
  - UAE Media Council / KSA GCAM Ad Licensing Requirements
- **Languages (BCP 47):** `ar, en` (RTL: `ar`)
- **Verified Date:** `2026-10-21`
