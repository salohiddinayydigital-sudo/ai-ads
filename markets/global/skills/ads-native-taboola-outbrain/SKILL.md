## Audit & Execution Checklist
1. Verify Server-to-Server (S2S) postback tracking (`click_id` / `tbclid` / `obOrigUrl`) rather than relying on third-party browser cookies.
2. Audit Publisher Site Blocklists: exclude low-time-on-site or zero-scroll-depth publisher placements after 2x target CPA spend with 0 micro-conversions.
3. Separate Desktop, Mobile Web, and Tablet campaigns (bid modifiers alone do not isolate publisher inventory quality).
