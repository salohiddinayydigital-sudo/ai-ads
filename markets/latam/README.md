# Market Pack: Latin America (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Chile) (`latam`)

- **Default Currency:** `BRL` (Supported: `BRL, MXN, COP, CLP, ARS, USD`)
- **Payment Methods:** Pix (Brazil — Instant), Cartao de Credito Parcelado (Installments), OXXO / SPEI (Mexico), Mercado Pago
- **BNPL Providers:** Mercado Credito, Parcelado sem juros (Up to 12x), Kueski Pay (MX), Addi (CO)
- **Price Anchor Template:** `Em ate 12x de {installment} sem juros ou 5% OFF no Pix`
- **Primary Funnels:**
  - Ad -> Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) -> Automated WhatsApp Flow / Sales Rep -> Pix / Payment Link
  - Ad -> E-Commerce / Mercado Libre -> Pix / Installment Checkout
- **Messaging Channels:** WhatsApp Business (95%+ reach), Instagram DM
- **Privacy & Ad Regulations:**
  - Brazil LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados, Law No. 13.709/2018)
  - CONAR (Brazil Advertising Self-Regulation)
  - Mexico LFPDPPP
- **Languages (BCP 47):** `pt-BR, es-MX, es-419, en` (RTL: `None`)
- **Verified Date:** `2026-10-21`
