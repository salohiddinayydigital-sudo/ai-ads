# Bozor paketi: Yevropa Ittifoqi va EIH (DE, FR, ES, IT, NL, PL, SE) (`eu`)

- **Standart valyuta:** `EUR` (qo'llab-quvvatlanadi: `EUR, PLN, SEK, DKK, CHF`)
- **To'lov usullari:** SEPA / Cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, iDEAL (NL), Bancontact (BE), BLIK (PL), Sofort / Giropay (DE)
- **BNPL provayderlar:** Klarna, PayPal Pay in 3/4, Scalapay, Alma
- **Narx anker shabloni:** `Pay in 3 interest-free installments of {installment} with {bnpl} (0% APR)`
- **Asosiy voronkalar:**
  - Reklama -> GDPR Cookie Banner (Consent Mode v2) -> Localized E-Commerce Store -> Klarna/PayPal Checkout
  - Reklama -> B2B Whitepaper / Webinar (Double Opt-In GDPR) -> HubSpot/Pipedrive -> Sales Call
- **Xabar kanallari:** WhatsApp Business, Email (Double Opt-In), Instagram DM
- **Maxfiylik va reklama qonunchiligi:**
  - EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) va ePrivacy Directive (Meta Pixel / CAPI PII ishga tushirishdan oldin oldindan opt-in roziligi)
  - Google Consent Mode v2 (EIHda ad_storage, analytics_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization majburiy)
  - EU Digital Markets Act (DMA) va Digital Services Act (DSA reklama shaffofligi)
  - EU Regulation 2024/900 — siyosiy reklama shaffofligi va targetingi to'g'risida
  - Omnibus Directive (chegirmalarda oldingi 30 kunlik eng past narxni ko'rsatish qoidasi)
- **Tillar (BCP 47):** `de, fr, es, it, nl, pl, sv, en` (RTL: `yo'q`)
- **Tekshirilgan sana:** `2026-10-21`
