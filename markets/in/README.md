# Market Pack: India (IN) (`in`)

- **Default Currency:** `INR` (Supported: `INR, USD`)
- **Payment Methods:** UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm — 75%+ digital volume), RuPay / Visa / Mastercard, NetBanking, Cash on Delivery (COD — with RTO protection)
- **BNPL Providers:** Simpl, LazyPay, Bajaj Finserv No-Cost EMI, Amazon Pay Later
- **Price Anchor Template:** `No-Cost EMI starting at {installment}/mo via {bnpl} or instant discount on UPI`
- **Primary Funnels:**
  - Ad -> D2C Store (Shopify + GoKwik / Shiprocket Checkout) -> UPI Pre-Paid Discount (to reduce COD RTO)
  - Ad -> Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) -> Automated WhatsApp Catalog / Lead Qualification -> CRM
- **Messaging Channels:** WhatsApp Business (Primary — 500M+ users), Instagram DM, SMS
- **Privacy & Ad Regulations:**
  - India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act)
  - ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) Code & Influencer Guidelines
  - SEBI / RBI Financial & Crypto Ad Restrictions
- **Languages (BCP 47):** `en-IN, hi, ta, te, mr, bn, kn` (RTL: `ur`)
- **Verified Date:** `2026-10-21`
