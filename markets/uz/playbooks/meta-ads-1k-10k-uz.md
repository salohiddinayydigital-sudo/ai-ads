# 🎯 META ADS $1,000–$10,000/OY BOSHQARUV PLAYBOOKI

## 1. Byudjet Taqsimoti (70 / 20 / 10 Qonuni)
- **70% (Scaling Core):** Isbotlangan Winner kreativlar va barqaror auditoriyalar (`Broad` yoki `1% Value LAL`).
- **20% (1-5-5 ABO Sandbox):** Haftalik yangi creative angle va hook sinovlari (har ad setda faqat 1 ta o'zgaruvchi).
- **10% (Retargeting & Warmup):** Issiq auditoriya (`IG DM 30d`, `Video 50%+ 14d`, `Website/Lead Form 14d`, `Purchasers 180d` exclude).

## 2. 4-Qatlamli Auditoriya va Exclusion Matritsasi
| Qatlam | Ulush | Maqsadli Qatlam | Majburiy Exclusions (Istisnolar) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1: Broad Andromeda** | 50% | Yosh + Jins + Geo (Toshkent / Viloyat alohida), qiziqishsiz | `Buyers 180d`, `Leads 30d`, `Active DM 14d` |
| **L2: High-Intent Stack** | 25% | 3–5 nisha interest + Solventnost (`Travelers`, `iPhone Pro`, `Admins`) | `Buyers 180d`, `Leads 30d`, `IG/FB Engagers 30d` |
| **L3: Value LAL (1%–2%)** | 15% | CRM LTV mijozlar bazasidan 1% va 1–2% Lookalike | `Buyers 180d`, `Leads 30d` |
| **L4: BOF Hot Retargeting** | 10% | `IG/FB Engagers 30d` + `Video 50% 14d` + `Web/Form 14d` | `Buyers 180d`, `Leads 7d` |

## 3. 30 Kunlik Ishga Tushirish Sikli
1. **1–3 kun (Texnik va Sandbox Start):** Pixel + CAPI deduplikatsiya (`event_id`), Break-even hisobi, 1-5-5 Sandbox ishga tushirish. **3 kun davomida ad set tahrirlanmaydi!**
2. **4–7 kun (Birinchi Saralash):** `Spend >= 1.5x Target CPA` bo'lib 0 lid bergan kreativlar o'chiriladi (`Kill`). `Hook Rate >= 30%` va `CPA <= Target` bergan kreativlar `Winner` deb belgilanadi.
3. **8–14 kun (Iteratsiya va Retargeting):** Winner kreativning 3 ta yangi Hook variatsiyasi yasaladi (`[[ad-iteration]]`), L4 Retargeting qatlami yoqiladi.
4. **15–30 kun (Vertikal va Gorizontal Scale):** Winner ad set byudjeti har 48–72 soatda **+20%** oshiriladi; `Overlap < 20%` va `FTIR >= 60%` doimiy nazorat qilinadi.
