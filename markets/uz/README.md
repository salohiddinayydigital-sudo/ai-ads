# Market Pack: Uzbekistan & Central Asia (O'zbekiston) (`uz`)

- **Default Currency:** `UZS` (Supported: `UZS, USD`)
- **Payment Methods:** Payme, Click, Uzcard, Humo, Visa/Mastercard
- **BNPL Providers:** Uzum Nasiya, Alif Nasiya, Anorbank Nasiya, Intend
- **Price Anchor Template:** `Oyiga atigi {installment} dan ({bnpl} orqali 12 oyga bo'lib to'lash)`
- **Primary Funnels:**
  - Ad -> Telegram Bot / Mini App -> AmoCRM / Bitrix24 -> Call Center (15-min SLA)
  - Ad -> Instagram Direct / Lead Form -> AmoCRM -> Sales Call
  - Ad -> Marketplace (Uzum Market) / Web Checkout (Payme/Click)
- **Messaging Channels:** Telegram (90%+ penetration), Instagram DM, SMS, WhatsApp
- **Privacy & Ad Regulations:**
  - O'zbekiston Respublikasining 'Shaxsga doir ma'lumotlar to'g'risida'gi Qonuni (O'RQ-547)
  - O'zbekiston Respublikasining 'Reklama to'g'risida'gi Qonuni (O'RQ-776)
- **Languages (BCP 47):** `uz-Latn, uz-Cyrl, ru, en` (RTL: `None`)
- **Verified Date:** `2026-10-21`
