# Loyiha Brifi: "Apex E-Commerce" (Demo Brand)

- **Loyiha nomi:** Apex E-Commerce (Kiyim-kechak va aksessuarlar e-commerce do'koni)
- **Bozor:** O'zbekiston & Markaziy Osiyo (Toshkent markazli)
- **Amaldagi kanallar:** Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook), Google Ads (Search + PMax), Telegram Ads (Kanal va Bot)
- **Asosiy maqsad:** Pullik media oqimini tartibga solish, keraksiz sarf-xarajatlarni (ad spend waste) 20%+ qisqartirish, Blended ROAS ni 1.8x dan 2.6x+ ga ko'tarish.

## 1. Iqtisodiy ko'rsatkichlar (Unit Economics)
- **O'rtacha chek (AOV):** 350,000 UZS (~$28)
- **Tannarx (COGS):** 120,000 UZS (~$9.6)
- **Yetkazib berish (Fulfillment):** 25,000 UZS
- **To'lov komissiyasi (Payme/Click):** 2.0% (7,000 UZS)
- **Sof yalpi foyda:** 198,000 UZS ($15.8)
- **Break-even CPA:** 198,000 UZS ($15.84)
- **Break-even ROAS:** 1.77x
- **Maqsadli ROAS (Target ROAS):** 2.50x+ (Target CPA: ~140,000 UZS / $11.20)

## 2. Platformalar bo'yicha joriy holat
| Platforma | Oylik byudjet | Joriy CPA | Joriy ROAS | Asosiy og'riq |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|------------|---------------|
| **Meta Ads** | $3,500/oy | $14.20 | 1.95x | Byudjet 8 ta mayda ad setga bo'lingan, o'rganish fazasidan chiqolmayapti, CAPI deduplikatsiyasi yo'q |
| **Google Ads** | $2,000/oy | $17.80 | 1.57x | Keng qidiruv (broad match) tufayli ~24% sarf axlat so'rovlarga ketmoqda, PMax organik brendni yutmoqda |
| **Telegram Ads** | $1,200/oy | $10.50 | 2.65x | Yaxshi ishlayapti, lekin bot onboarding sekin (18 soniya), kreativlar 1 oyda charchagan |

## 3. Muvaffaqiyat mezoni (DoD)
1. Meta da 1-5-5 CBO konsolidatsiyasi orqali ad setlar sonini 8 tadan 2 taga qisqartirish.
2. Google Ads da 45 ta salbiy kalit so'z (Negative Keywords) qo'shish va PMax ga Brand Exclusion List o'rnatish.
3. Telegram Ads da deep linking (`?start=c_utm`) va 160 belgili yangi 3 ta burchakni kiritish.
4. Barcha o'zgarishlar Teskari qadam (Rollback) ga ega Change Packet orqali amalga oshirilishi shart.
