# Meta Ads sozlamalari

## Kampaniya qurilmasi

- **Maqsad (Objective):** Leads
- **Konversiya joyi:** Website (Landing page)
- **Optimizatsiya hodisasi (Optimization Event):** Lead yoki Schedule

## Targeting: Broad

- Faqat yosh **29–64** + lokatsiya **25–35 milya radius**
- **Qiziqishlar (interests) tanlanmaydi** — kreativning o'zi auditoriyani saralaydi: *messaging is targeting*

## Placements

- Faqat **Facebook Feed + Instagram Feed**
- Audience Network va boshqa "axlat" placement'lar o'chiriladi

## Byudjet

- Bitta adset uchun **$30–$50 / kun**
- **Oshirish cheklovi:** kuniga **20–30% dan oshirmaslik** (amalda: har 2–3 kunda 20–30%) — katta sakrash algoritmni **learning phase**ga qaytaradi, natija narxi oshib ketadi

## O'lchov (DoD)

Pixel'da **Lead** va **Schedule** hodisalari to'g'ri o'lchanishi shart.
