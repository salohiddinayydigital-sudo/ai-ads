# Funnel filtr qoidalari

> Bog'lanishlar: [[survey-funnel]] · [[mijoz-ishga-tushirish]] · [[content-is-targeting]]

## Mazmun
- Sovuq traffic'da lead sifatining past bo'lishi tabiiy ("welcome to cold traffic") — sales process leadni yaratmaydi, filtrlaydi ("filters for good ones").
- Filter narxi: yaxshi filter qo'shilsa lead qimmatlashadi lekin sifati oshadi; yomon filter qo'shilsa qimmatlashadi lekin sifati oshmaydi. Sekin yuklanadigan sahifa — yomon friction; VSL (video sales letter) — yaxshi friction, lead sifatini oshiradi.
- Perfect funnel sozlamasi: opt-in ko'p lekin sifatsiz bo'lsa → friction qo'shish; opt-in kam bo'lsa → friction olib tashlash; friction deyarli yo'q bo'lsa ham leadlar yomon bo'lsa → muammo targetingda.
- Local biznes — ads uchun eng oson: trust sky-high, shuning uchun brand o'rniga offer-driven yuritiladi (acupuncture misoli: $19-29 intro offer, opt-in → thank-you page'da xarid, card on file, appointment confirm, davolash → keyin uzoq muddatli plan sotish; Hormozi davolashdan oldin sotishni afzal ko'radi).
- Targeting boshlanishi: mavjud customer list / email list (1000+)dan lookalike audience; copy va videoda auditoriya tilida gapirilsa (masalan "dentist" pain pointlari) Meta AI ham reklamaning kim uchun ekanini sezib to'g'ri audiencega beradi.
