# Lead magnetni yetkazib berish va nomlash

> Bog'lanishlar: [[lead-magnet-turlari]] · [[taklif-tuzilishi]] · [[kreativ-hit-rate]]

## Mazmun
- Yetkazib berishning 4 usuli (kombinatsiya qilish mumkin):
  - Software/tools — hisob-kitob spreadsheets, assessment tool, template, mini software (Neil Patel'ning URL audit tool'i). Boshqa 3 turni ham fulfill qiladi.
  - Information — mini kurs, guide, expert interview: arzon, cheksiz scalable, operational drag yo'q, deprivation yaratadi (Hormozi'ning $100M scaling roadmap'i — tool+information kombinatsiyasi).
  - Services — bepul ish qilib berish (audit + implementation, same-day delivery, done-for-you); faqat qualify qilinganlarga (budget, authority, need, timing bo'lganlar). Matematika: 1 soatlik ish hard cost $25, bozor qiymati $250; 4 kishiga qilib 1 tisi xarid qilsa — CAC $100.
  - Physical — kitoblar ham lead magnet; conference'da $3-4 lik "CEO" futbolka faqat CEO ekanini isbotlaganga berilsa — sifatli CEO ro'yxati; 20 tadan 1 konversiya bo'lsa $100/CEO.
- Nomlash engagement rate'ni boshqa hamma narsadan ko'proq belgilaydi: mazmunni o'zgartirmasdan faqat headline bilan talabni 2x, 3x, 10x ga o'zgartirish mumkin — shuning uchun wrapping/packaging ichidagidan ko'proq test qilinadi.
- Vehicle emas, result reklama qilinadi: "6-week deadlift and squat seminar" emas — "Big booty boot camp" (ayni mashg'ulot, boshqa nom).
- Nomni topishning siri — auditoriyadan so'rash: kichik ad testlari (headline'lar solishtiriladi), stories poll, comment "A yoki B", SMS; poll oldidan auditoriyaga kirmaganlar javob bermasligi aytildi. $100M Leads kitobi nomi uchun 6 ta headline, image va subheadline split test qilingan.
- Naming konvensiyalari:
  - raqam + natija + muddat: "3 emails that turn cold leads into clients in 24 hours"
  - how to X without Y (eng katta xavotir): "how to build a funnel without hiring a copywriter"
  - sifat + kerakli narsa: "the lazy funnel template that converts like crazy"
  - X mistakes: "5 ad copy mistakes that are killing your conversion rate"
- Lead magnet oldida, ichida va keyin CTA bo'lishi kerak; CTA formulasi — clear, not clever + exact next action + reason to do now (scarcity/urgency yoki oddiy "sabab": hatto mantiqsiz sabab ham sababsizlikdan yaxshi ishlaydi).
- Ko'p so'rash kerak, lekin so'rashlar orasida value berib turish orqali ("value per second" oshirish) — aks holda auditoriya so'rash imkonini berishni to'xtatadi. Service biznesda (bizneslarning 78%) capacity cheklovi ochiq aytib scarcity qilinadi; katta biznes rolling cohort bilan cheklaydi.
