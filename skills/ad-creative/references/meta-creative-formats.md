# Meta & Short-Form Ad Format Taxonomy (2026 S->F Tier List)

Grounded in the [2026 Meta Andromeda & GEM Architecture Guide](https://greghal.no/en/blog/meta-ads-algorithm-2026-complete-guide/) and [Linear Design Advantage+ Creative Study](https://lineardesign.com/blog/metas-advantage-audience/).

## Why Format Diversity Drives Retrieval (The Entity ID Rule)
Meta's **Andromeda** retrieval engine uses computer vision to group visually similar creatives under a single **Entity ID** (1 retrieval ticket). Uploading 20 hook text swaps over the same video gives you **1 ticket**. To earn 10 distinct retrieval tickets, combine **Persona-Desire-Awareness (PDA)** shifts with distinct formats from Tiers S and A below:

| Tier | Format Name | Media Type | Funnel Role | Why It Works in Andromeda / GEM / UTIS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S-Tier** | **1. Founder / Expert Direct-to-Camera Authority** | `9:16` Video (`25-45s`) | Unicorn Cold-Scaler (TOF/MOF) | Highest trust & UTIS genuine-interest score; reports 2–3x ROAS lift over polished studio ads. |
| **S-Tier** | **2. Reaction + Hard-Cut Product Demo (UGC)** | `9:16` Video (`15-30s`) | Unicorn Cold-Scaler (TOF) | Opens with a 2-second visceral reaction/problem, hard-cuts immediately into live product proof. |
| **S-Tier** | **3. Partnership / Whitelisted Creator Ad** | `9:16` Video / `4:5` | Unicorn Cold-Scaler (TOF) | Running from a creator's handle generates a brand-new Andromeda **Entity ID** and fresh audience trajectory. |
| **S-Tier** | **4. "No Yapping" Split-Screen Tutorial / Demo** | `9:16` Video (`15-25s`) | Cold & Warm Scaler | Top half: real-time product/software action; bottom half: creator explaining exact numeric payoff in `<20s`. |
| **S-Tier** | **5. Mini-VSL (Problem -> Mechanism -> Proof -> Offer)** | `9:16` / `4:5` Video (`45-90s`) | High-Ticket / Lead-Gen Scaler | Qualifies high-intent buyers in Real Estate, Medical, EdTech, and B2B before they click. |
| **A-Tier** | **6. iOS-Native Reveal (iMessage / ChatGPT / Notes)** | `9:16` Video (`15-25s`) | Cold Pattern-Interrupt | Unfolds bubble-by-bubble (`Screenshot hook -> "What app/clinic is that?" -> Brand + Offer reveal`). |
| **A-Tier** | **7. Greenscreen Comment / Objection Reply** | `9:16` Video (`20-35s`) | MOF/BOF Crusher | Creator stands in front of a real customer comment/objection or pricing breakdown and answers with proof. |
| **A-Tier** | **8. Faceless Motion-Style Explainer** | `9:16` / `4:5` Video (`15-35s`) | Cold & B2B/SaaS Scaler | Swiss typographic, flat vector, or duotone collage explainer with word-timed captions and zero actor cost. |
| **A-Tier** | **9. Us vs. Them Comparison Card** | `4:5` Static / Carousel | Cold & Warm Converter | Side-by-side visual contrast of the old painful alternative vs. your mechanism + BNPL price anchor. |
| **A-Tier** | **10. Verified Review / Stat Callout / FAQ Card** | `4:5` Static | MOF/BOF Retargeting | Turns top customer review quotes and comment objections into scannable 2-second static proof cards. |
| **B-Tier** | **11. ASMR Unboxing / Process POV & Street Interview** | `9:16` Video (`15-30s`) | Supporting Cast | Strong thumbstop for sensory categories (Beauty, Auto Detailing, Food/HoReCa, Fashion). |
| **F-Tier** | **12. Fake-Native Slop (Fake Podcast / Fake Press / Clickbait)** | Video / Static | **Avoid (UTIS Penalty)** | Demoted by Meta's **UTIS (User True Interest Survey)** model when viewers report low genuine interest match. |
