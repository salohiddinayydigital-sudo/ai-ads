# No-AI-Tells Copywriting Filter & Falsifiable Test Hypotheses

## 1. Banned AI Copywriting Tells (Never Output These)
1. **Contrast Reveals:** *"It's not X, it's Y"*, *"Bu shunchaki X emas — bu Y"*, *"Не просто X, а Y"*. State the concrete benefit directly.
2. **Stacked Negations:** *"No hidden fees. No delays. No stress."* Keep at most one concrete risk-reversal fact (*"0% muddatli to'lov, boshlang'ich to'lovsiz"*).
3. **Hook Slop:** *"Stop scrolling!"*, *"Wait before you scroll!"*, *"O'tkazib yubormang!"*, *"Не пролистывай!"*. Replace with a concrete buyer problem or quantified proof.
4. **Generic CTA Slop:** *"Get started today"*, *"Unlock the power of X"*, *"Take your business to the next level"*. State the exact action and immediate reward (*"Bepul 3D diagnostika uchun '+' qoldiring"* / *"Start your 14-day free trial — setup takes 4 minutes"*).
5. **Typography Tells:** Never use em-dashes (`—`) in ad headlines or primary text; use periods, commas, or line breaks.

## 2. Falsifiable Test Hypothesis Contract
Every creative concept or test group must include a hypothesis that real campaign data can prove **wrong**:
- **Required Formula:** `In [Target Audience / Layer], [Concept / Format] will beat [Control / Baseline] on [Primary Metric: 3s Hook Rate | Outbound CTR | CPA | ROAS] by [Expected Direction & Magnitude] because [Psychological / Visual Mechanism].`
